The COSTCO management method

February 1st, 2026

Engineering management is easy. All it takes is providing six simple things to your engineers: context, order, space, tools, connections, and opportunity.

Introducing the COSTCO system.

(No association with the Costco Wholesale Company)

Context

Order

More often called priority in the corporate world, but CPSTCO just doesn't have the same ring to it.

Order is about ensuring your engineers have a clear understanding of their project priorities. No matter how experienced, it can be difficult for engineers to context switch between multiple projects at the same time. My goal is to provide a prioritized list of projects for each of my engineers and then space to focus on the top priority without interruption, more on that next. My current team is highly experienced and does work for 7 product managers, meaning I focus more on providing project priorities that managing ticket priority within a sprint, but the importance of order can apply at any level of granularity. It'll just depend on the team's need.

For the Staff engineers on my team, I have slightly higher expectations for context switching, typtically prioritizing one development project and one planning project, but in general the engineers shouldn't need to be switching between development context.
